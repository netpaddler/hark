No. 76

Praise, My Soul, the King of Heaven

Henry F. Lyte

LAUDA ANIMA · John Goss

1834; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Praise, my soul, the King of heaven,to his feet your tribute bring;ransomed, healed, restored, forgiven,who, like me, his praise should sing?Praise him, praise him, praise him, praise him,praise the everlasting King.

Stanza 2

Praise him for his grace and favorto our fathers in distress;praise him, still the same forever,slow to chide and swift to bless;praise him, praise him, praise him, praise him,glorious in his faithfulness.

Stanza 3

Father-like, he tends and spares us;well our feeble frame he knows;in his hands he gently bears us,rescues us from all our foes;praise him, praise him, praise him, praise him,widely as his mercy goes.

Stanza 4

Frail as summer's flow'r we flourish,blows the wind and it is gone;but while mortals rise and perish,God endures unchanging on.Praise him, praise him, praise him, praise him,praise the High Eternal One.

Stanza 5

Angels, help us to adore him;you behold him face to face;sun and moon, bow down before him,dwellers all in time and space,praise him, praise him, praise him, praise him,praise with us the God of grace.
