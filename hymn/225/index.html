No. 225

Once in Royal David's City

Cecil F. Alexander

IRBY · Henry J. Gauntlett

text 1848

Themes: Christmas, The Incarnation, Christ's Deity, The Second Coming, Grace and Redemption

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Once in royal David's citystood a lowly cattle shed,where a mother laid her babyin a manger for his bed:Mary was that mother mild,Jesus Christ her little child.

Stanza 2

He came down to earth from heavenwho is God and Lord of all,and his shelter was a stable,and his cradle was a stall:with the poor, and mean, and lowly,lived on earth our Savior holy.

Stanza 3

And through all his wondrous childhoodhe would honor and obey,love and watch the lowly maidenin whose gentle arms he lay:Christian children all must bemild, obedient, good as he.

Stanza 4

And our eyes at last shall see him,through his own redeeming love;for that child so dear and gentleis our Lord in heav'n above,and he leads his children onto the place where he is gone.

Stanza 5

Not in that poor lowly stable,with the oxen standing by,we shall see him, but in heaven,set at God's right hand on high;when like stars his children crownedall in white shall wait around.
