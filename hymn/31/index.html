No. 31

Have You Not Known, Have You Not Heard

Isaac Watts

HERMON · Lowell Mason

1707; alt. in Scottish Paraphrases, 1781; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Have you not known, have you not heardthat firm remains on highthe everlasting throne of himwho formed the earth and sky?

Stanza 2

Are you afraid his pow'r shall failwhen comes your evil day?And can an all-creating armgrow weary or decay?

Stanza 3

Supreme in wisdom as in pow'rthe Rock of Ages stands,though him you cannot see, nor tracethe working of his hands.

Stanza 4

He gives the conquest to the weak,supports the fainting heart;and courage in the evil hourhis heav'nly aids impart.

Stanza 5

Mere human pow'r shall fast decay,and youthful vigor cease;but they who wait upon the Lordin strength shall still increase.

Stanza 6

They with unwearied feet shall treadthe path of life divine;with growing ardor onward move,with growing brightness shine.

Stanza 7

On eagles' wings they mount, they soar—their wings are faith and love—till, past the cloudy regions here,they rise to heav'n above.
