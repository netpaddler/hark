No. 404

Sun of My Soul, Thou Savior Dear

John Keble

HURSLEY

1820

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Sun of my soul, thou Savior dear,it is not night if thou be near;O may no earthborn cloud ariseto hide thee from thy servant's eyes.

Stanza 2

When the soft dews of kindly sleepmy weary eyelids gently steep,be my last thought, how sweet to restforever on my Savior's breast.

Stanza 3

Abide with me from morn till eve,for without thee I cannot live;abide with me when night is nigh,for without thee I dare not die.

Stanza 4

If some poor wand'ring child of thinehave spurned today the voice divine,now, Lord, the gracious work begin;let him no more lie down in sin.

Stanza 5

Watch by the sick; enrich the poorwith blessings from thy boundless store;be every mourner's sleep tonight,like infant's slumbers, pure and light.

Stanza 6

Come near and bless us when we wake,ere through the world our way we take,till in the ocean of thy lovewe lose ourselves in heav'n above.
