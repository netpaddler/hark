Timeless songs of grace

No. 472

Come, Ye Sinners, Poor and Wretched

Joseph Hart

BRYN CALFARIA · William Owen

1759; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Come, ye sinners, poor and wretched,weak and wounded, sick and sore;Jesus ready stands to save you,full of pity joined with pow'r:he is able, he is able, he is able,he is willing; doubt no more;he is willing; doubt no more.

Stanza 2

Come, ye needy, come and welcome,God's free bounty glorify;true belief and true repentance,ev'ry grace that brings you nigh,without money, without money, without money,come to Jesus Christ and buy;come to Jesus Christ and buy.

Stanza 3

Come, ye weary, heavy laden,bruised and broken by the fall;if you tarry till you're better,you will never come at all:not the righteous, not the righteous, not the righteous—sinners Jesus came to call;sinners Jesus came to call.

Stanza 4

Let not conscience make you linger,nor of fitness fondly dream;all the fitness he requirethis to feel your need of him;this he gives you, this he gives you, this he gives you;'tis the Spirit's rising beam;'tis the Spirit's rising beam.

Stanza 5

Lo! th'incarnate God, ascended,pleads the merit of his blood;venture on him, venture wholly,let no other trust intrude:none but Jesus, none but Jesus, none but Jesuscan do helpless sinners good,can do helpless sinners good.
