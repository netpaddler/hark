The Lord's my Shepherd, I'll not want;he makes me down to liein pastures green; he leadeth methe quiet waters by.He leadeth me, he leadeth methe quiet waters by.

My soul he doth restore again;and me to walk doth makewithin the paths of righteousness,e'en for his own name's sake;within the paths of righteousness,e'en for his own name's sake.
