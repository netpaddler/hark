No. 213

What Child Is This

William C. Dix (adapter)

GREENSLEEVES · English melody, 16th cent.

text ca. 1865

Themes: Christmas, Epiphany, The Incarnation, Christ the King, The Magi and the Nations, Grace and Redemption

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

What child is this, who, laid to rest,on Mary's lap is sleeping?Whom angels greet with anthems sweet,while shepherds watch are keeping?This, this is Christ the King,whom shepherds guard and angels sing:haste, haste to bring him laud,the babe, the son of Mary.

Stanza 2

Why lies he in such mean estate,where ox and ass are feeding?Good Christian, fear; for sinners herethe silent Word is pleading.Nails, spear, shall pierce him through;the cross be borne for me, for you:hail, hail the Word made flesh,the babe, the son of Mary.

Stanza 3

So bring him incense, gold, and myrrh;come, peasant, king, to own him;the King of kings salvation brings,let loving hearts enthrone him.Raise, raise the song on high,the virgin sings her lullaby:joy, joy for Christ is born,the babe, the son of Mary.
