Timeless songs of grace

No. 198

Lift Up Your Heads, Ye Mighty Gates!

Georg Weissel · tr. Catherine Winkworth

TRURO

text 1642; tr. 1855, alt.

Themes: Advent, Christ the King, Longing and Hope

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Lift up your heads, ye mighty gates!Behold, the King of glory waits;the King of kings is drawing near,the Savior of the world is here.

Stanza 2

A helper just he comes to thee,his chariot is humility,his kingly crown is holiness,his scepter, pity in distress.

Stanza 3

O blest the land, the city blest,where Christ the Ruler is confessed!O happy hearts and happy homesto whom this King in triumph comes!

Stanza 4

Fling wide the portals of your heart;make it a temple, set apartfrom earthly use for heav'n's employ,adorned with prayer and love and joy.

Stanza 5

Redeemer, come! I open widemy heart to thee; here, Lord, abide!Let me thy inner presence feel;thy grace and love in me reveal.

Stanza 6

So come, my Sovereign, enter in!Let new and nobler life begin!Thy Holy Spirit, guide us on,until the glorious crown be won.
