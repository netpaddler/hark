No. 296

All Hail the Power of Jesus' Name

Edward Perronet (st. 1-5); John Rippon (st. 6)

CORONATION · Oliver Holden (1793)

st. 1-5, 1779; alt.; st. 6, 1787

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

All hail the pow'r of Jesus' name!Let angels prostrate fall;bring forth the royal diadem,and crown him Lord of all;bring forth the royal diadem,and crown him Lord of all.

Stanza 2

Crown him, ye martyrs of your God,who from his altar call;extol the Stem of Jesse's rod,and crown him Lord of all;extol the Stem of Jesse's rod,and crown him Lord of all.

Stanza 3

Ye seed of Israel's chosen race,ye ransomed of the fall,hail him who saves you by his grace,and crown him Lord of all;hail him who saves you by his grace,and crown him Lord of all.

Stanza 4

Sinners, whose love can ne'er forgetthe wormwood and the gall,go, spread your trophies at his feet,and crown him Lord of all;go, spread your trophies at his feet,and crown him Lord of all.

Stanza 5

Let ev'ry kindred, ev'ry tribe,on this terrestrial ball,to him all majesty ascribe,and crown him Lord of all;to him all majesty ascribe,and crown him Lord of all.

Stanza 6

O that with yonder sacred throngwe at his feet may fall;we'll join the everlasting song,and crown him Lord of all;we'll join the everlasting song,and crown him Lord of all.
