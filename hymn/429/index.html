Timeless songs of grace

No. 429

Let Thy Blood in Mercy Poured

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Let thy blood in mercy poured,let thy gracious body broken,be to me, O gracious Lord,of thy boundless love the token.

Refrain

Thou didst give thyself for me,now I give myself to thee.

Stanza 2

Thou didst die that I might live;blessed Lord, thou cam'st to save me;all that love of God could give,Jesus by his sorrows gave me.

Refrain

Thou didst give thyself for me,now I give myself to thee.

Stanza 3

By the thorns that crowned thy brow,by the spear wound and the nailing,by the pain and death, I nowclaim, O Christ, thy love unfailing.

Refrain

Thou didst give thyself for me,now I give myself to thee.

Stanza 4

Wilt thou own the gift I bring?All my penitence I give thee;thou art my exalted King,of thy matchless love forgive me.

Refrain

Thou didst give thyself for me,now I give myself to thee.
