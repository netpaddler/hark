No. 566

Fear Not, O Little Flock

Johann Michael Altenburg (attributed) · tr. Catherine Winkworth

JEHOVAH NISSI · Edward Patrick Crawford (1846-1912)

1584-1640; tr. 1855; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Fear not, O little flock, the foewho madly seeks your overthrow;dread not his rage and pow'r:what though your courage sometimes faints,his seeming triumph o'er God's saintslasts but a little hour.

Stanza 2

Be of good cheer; your cause belongsto him who can avenge your wrongs;leave it to him, our Lord:though hidden yet from all our eyes,he sees the Gideon who shall riseto save us and his Word.

Stanza 3

As true as God's own Word is true,nor earth nor hell with all their crewagainst us shall prevail.A jest and byword are they grown;God is with us, we are his own;our vict'ry cannot fail.

Stanza 4

Amen, Lord Jesus, grant our pray'r;great Captain, now your arm make bare,fight for us once again;so shall your saints and martyrs raisea mighty chorus to your praise,world without end. Amen.
