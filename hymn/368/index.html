No. 368

The Ends of All the Earth Shall Hear

The Psalter

VISION · William H. Doane

1912

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

The ends of all the earth shall hearand turn unto the Lord in fear;all kindreds of the earth shall ownand worship him as God alone.

Refrain

All earth to him her homage brings,the Lord of lords, the King of kings.

Stanza 2

For his the kingdom, his of right,he rules the nations by his might;all earth to him her homage brings,the Lord of Lords, the King of kings.

Refrain

All earth to him her homage brings,the Lord of lords, the King of kings.

Stanza 3

Both rich and poor, both bond and free,shall worship him on bended knee,and children's children shall proclaimthe glorious honor of his name.

Refrain

All earth to him her homage brings,the Lord of lords, the King of kings.
