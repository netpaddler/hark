No. 359

Blest Be the Tie That Binds

John Fawcett

DENNIS · Hans G. Nägeli (1773-1836); arr. Lowell Mason (1845)

1782

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Blest be the tie that bindsour hearts in Christian love:the fellowship of kindred mindsis like to that above.

Stanza 2

Before the Father's thronewe pour our ardent prayers;our fears, our hopes, our aims are one,our comforts and our cares.

Stanza 3

We share our mutual woes,our mutual burdens bear,and often for each other flowsthe sympathizing tear.

Stanza 4

When we asunder part,it gives us inward pain;but we shall still be joined in heart,and hope to meet again.

Stanza 5

This glorious hope revivesour courage by the way,while each in expectation lives,and longs to see the day.

Stanza 6

From sorrow, toil and pain,and sin, we shall be free;and perfect love and friendship reignthrough all eternity.
