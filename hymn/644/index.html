No. 644

May the Mind of Christ My Savior

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

May the mind of Christ my Saviorlive in me from day to day,by his love and pow'r controllingall I do and say.

Stanza 2

May the Word of God dwell richlyin my heart from hour to hour,so that all may see I triumphonly through his pow'r.

Stanza 3

May the peace of God my Fatherrule my life in everything,that I may be calm to comfortsick and sorrowing.

Stanza 4

May the love of Jesus fill meas the waters fill the sea;him exalting, self abasing,this is victory.

Stanza 5

May his beauty rest upon meas I seek the lost to win,and may they forget the channel,seeing only him.
