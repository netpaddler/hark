No. 424

Shepherd of Souls, Refresh and Bless

Anon. (st. 1-3); James Montgomery (st. 4-5)

ST. AGNES · John B. Dykes (1866)

st. 4-5: 1825; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Shepherd of souls, refresh and blessyour chosen pilgrim flockwith manna in the wilderness,with water from the rock.

Stanza 2

Hungry and thirsty, faint and weak,as you when here below,our souls the joys celestial seekwhich from your sorrows flow.

Stanza 3

We would not live by bread alone,but by that Word of grace,in strength of which we travel onto our abiding place.

Stanza 4

Be known to us in breaking bread,but do not then depart;Savior, abide with us, and spreadyour table in our heart.

Stanza 5

There sup with us in love divine;your body and your blood,that living bread, that heav'nly wine,be our immortal food.
