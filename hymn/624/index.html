No. 624

Through All the Changing Scenes of Life

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Through all the changing scenes of life,in trouble and in joy,the praises of my God shall stillmy heart and tongue employ.

Stanza 2

Of his deliv'rance I will boast,till all that are distressedfrom my example comfort take,and charm their griefs to rest.

Stanza 3

O magnify the Lord with me,with me exalt his name;when in distress to him I called,he to my rescue came.

Stanza 4

The hosts of God encamp aroundthe dwellings of the just;deliv'rance he affords to allwho on his succor trust.

Stanza 5

O make but trial of his love;experience will decidehow blest they are, and only they,who in his truth confide.

Stanza 6

Fear him, ye saints, and you will thenhave nothing else to fear:make you his service your delight;he'll make your wants his care.
