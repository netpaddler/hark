No. 193

Let All Mortal Flesh Keep Silence

Liturgy of St. James, 5th cent. · tr. Gerard Moultrie (para.)

PICARDY · French melody, 17th cent.; arr. Ralph Vaughan Williams (1906)

Greek liturgy 5th cent.; para. 1864

Themes: Advent, The Incarnation, Christ's Deity, Christ the King

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Let all mortal flesh keep silence,and with fear and trembling stand;ponder nothing earthly-minded,for with blessing in his hand,Christ our God to earth descendeth,our full homage to demand.

Stanza 2

King of kings, yet born of Mary,as of old on earth he stood;Lord of lords, in human vesture,in the body and the blood,he will give to all the faithfulhis own self for heav'nly food.

Stanza 3

Rank on rank the host of heavenspreads its vanguard on the way,as the Light of light descendethfrom the realms of endless day,that the pow'rs of hell may vanishas the darkness clears away.

Stanza 4

At his feet the six-winged seraph;cherubim, with sleepless eye,veil their faces to the presence,as with ceaseless voice they cry,"Alleluia, alleluia,alleluia, Lord Most High!"
