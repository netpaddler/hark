No. 708

O Love That Will Not Let Me Go

George Matheson

ST. MARGARET · Albert L. Peace (1885)

1882

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

O Love that wilt not let me go,I rest my weary soul in thee;I give thee back the life I owe,that in thine ocean depths its flowmay richer, fuller be.

Stanza 2

O Light that follow'st all my way,I yield my flick'ring torch to thee;my heart restores its borrowed ray,that in thy sunshine's blaze its daymay brighter, fairer be.

Stanza 3

O Joy that seekest me through pain,I cannot close my heart to thee;I trace the rainbow through the rain,and feel the promise is not vainthat morn shall tearless be.

Stanza 4

O Cross that liftest up my head,I dare not ask to fly from thee;I lay in dust life's glory dead,and from the ground there blossoms redlife that shall endless be.
