No. 520

Jesus, Thy Blood and Righteousness

Nikolaus Ludwig von Zinzendorf · tr. John Wesley (1740)

GERMANY · William Gardiner (1815)

1739; tr. 1740; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Jesus, thy blood and righteousnessmy beauty are, my glorious dress;'midst flaming worlds, in these arrayed,with joy shall I lift up my head.

Stanza 2

Bold shall I stand in thy great day;for who aught to my charge shall lay?Fully absolved through these I am,from sin and fear, from guilt and shame.

Stanza 3

When from the dust of death I riseto claim my mansion in the skies,ev'n then this shall be all my plea,Jesus hath lived, hath died, for me.

Stanza 4

Jesus, be endless praise to thee,whose boundless mercy hath for me,for me a full atonement made,an everlasting ransom paid.

Stanza 5

O let the dead now hear thy voice;now bid thy banished ones rejoice;their beauty this, their glorious dress,Jesus, thy blood and righteousness.
