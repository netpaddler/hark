No. 615

Come, Ye Disconsolate, Where'er Ye Languish

Thomas Moore

ALMA · Samuel Webbe

1816; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Come, ye disconsolate, where'er ye languish,come to the mercy seat, fervently kneel:here bring your wounded hearts, here tell your anguish;earth has no sorrows that heav'n cannot heal.

Stanza 2

Joy of the comfortless, light of the straying,hope of the penitent, fadeless and pure!Here speaks the Comforter, in mercy saying,"Earth has no sorrows that heav'n cannot cure."

Stanza 3

Here see the Bread of Life; see waters flowingforth from the throne of God, pure from above:come to the feast prepared; come, ever knowingearth has no sorrows but heav'n can remove.
