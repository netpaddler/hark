No. 294

The Golden Gates Are Lifted Up

Cecil Frances Alexander

MIRFIELD · Arthur Cottman (1872)

1852, 1858

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

The golden gates are lifted up,the doors are opened wide;the King of glory is gone inunto his Father's side.

Stanza 2

Thou art gone up before us, Lord,to make for us a place,that we may be where now thou art,and look upon God's face.

Stanza 3

And ever on our earthly patha gleam of glory lies,a light still breaks behind the cloudthat veiled thee from our eyes.

Stanza 4

Lift up our hearts, lift up our minds:let thy dear grace be giv'n,that, while we wander here below,our treasure be in heav'n;

Stanza 5

That where thou art, at God's right hand,our hope, our love, may be:dwell thou in us, that we may dwellforevermore in thee.
