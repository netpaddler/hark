No. 207

Good Christian Men, Rejoice

Medieval Latin carol · tr. John Mason Neale

IN DULCI JUBILO · German melody, 14th cent.; arr. W. D.

tr. 1853; alt.; alt. 1961

Themes: Christmas, Joy and Praise, Grace and Redemption

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Good Christian men, rejoice,with heart and soul and voice;give ye heed to what we say:Jesus Christ is born today;earth and heav'n before him bow,and he is in the manger now.Christ is born today!Christ is born today!

Stanza 2

Good Christian men, rejoice,with heart and soul and voice;now ye hear of endless bliss:Jesus Christ was born for this!He hath opened heaven's door,and man is blessed evermore.Christ was born for this!Christ was born for this!

Stanza 3

Good Christian men, rejoice,with heart and soul and voice;now ye need not fear the grave:Jesus Christ was born to save!Calls you one and calls you allto gain his everlasting hall.Christ was born to save!Christ was born to save!
