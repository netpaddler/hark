No. 706

Jesus Lives, and So Shall I

Christian F. Gellert · tr. J. D. Lang

JESUS, MEINE ZUVERSICHT · Johann Crüger (1653); arr.

1757; tr. 1826

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Jesus lives, and so shall I.Death! thy sting is gone forever!He who deigned for me to die,lives, the bands of death to sever;He shall raise me from the dust:Jesus is my hope and trust.

Stanza 2

Jesus lives, and reigns supreme;and, his kingdom still remaining,I shall also be with him,ever living, ever reigning.God has promised; be it must:Jesus is my hope and trust.

Stanza 3

Jesus lives, and by his gracevict'ry o'er my passions giving,I will cleanse my heart and ways,ever to his glory living.Me he raises from the dust:Jesus is my hope and trust.

Stanza 4

Jesus lives! I know full wellnaught from him my heart can sever,life nor death nor pow'rs of hell,joy nor grief, henceforth forever.None of all his saints is lost:Jesus is my hope and trust.

Stanza 5

Jesus lives and death is nowbut my entrance into glory.Courage, then, my soul, for thouhast a crown of life before thee;thou shalt find thy hopes were just:Jesus is the Christian's trust.
