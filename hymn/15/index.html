No. 15

Stand Up and Bless the Lord

James Montgomery

CARLISLE · Charles Lockhart

1824, 1825; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Stand up and bless the Lord,you people of his choice;stand up and bless the Lord your Godwith heart and soul and voice.

Stanza 2

Though high above all praise,above all blessing high,who would not fear his holy name,and laud and magnify?

Stanza 3

O for the living flamefrom his own altar brought,to touch our lips, our minds inspire,and wing to heav'n our thought!

Stanza 4

God is our strength and song,and his salvation ours;then be his love in Christ proclaimedwith all our ransomed pow'rs.

Stanza 5

Stand up and bless the Lord,the Lord your God adore;stand up and bless his glorious namehenceforth forevermore.
