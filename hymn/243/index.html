No. 243

Praise the Savior Now and Ever

Venantius H. C. Fortunatus (ca. 530-609) · tr. Augustus Nelson (1863-1949)

UPP, MIN TUNGA

ca. 530-609; tr. 1863-1949

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Praise the Savior now and ever;praise him, all beneath the skies;prostrate lying, suf'fring, dyingon the cross, a sacrifice.Vict'ry gaining, life obtaining,now in glory he doth rise.

Stanza 2

Man's work faileth, Christ's availeth;he is all our righteousness;he, our Savior, has foreverset us free from dire distress.Through his merit we inheritlight and peace and happiness.

Stanza 3

Sin's bond severed, we're delivered;Christ has bruised the serpent's head;death no longer is the stronger;hell itself is captive led.Christ has risen from death's prison;o'er the tomb he light has shed.

Stanza 4

For his favor, praise foreverunto God the Father sing;praise the Savior, praise him ever,Son of God, our Lord and King.Praise the Spirit; through Christ's merithe doth us salvation bring.
