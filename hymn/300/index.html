No. 300

Blessing and Honor and Glory and Power

Horatius Bonar

O QUANTA QUALIA · La Feillée's Méthode du plain-chant (1808)

1866; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Blessing and honor and glory and power,wisdom and riches and strength evermoregive ye to him who our battle hath won,whose are the kingdom, the crown, and the throne.

Stanza 2

Soundeth the heav'n of the heav'ns with his name;ringeth the earth with his glory and fame;ocean and mountain, stream, forest, and flowerecho his praises and tell of his power.

Stanza 3

Ever ascendeth the song and the joy;ever descendeth the love from on high;blessing and honor and glory and praise,this is the theme of the hymns that we raise.

Stanza 4

Give we the glory and praise to the Lamb;take we the robe and the harp and the palm;sing we the song of the Lamb that was slain,dying in weakness, but rising to reign.
