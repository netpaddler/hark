No. 567

Rise, My Soul, to Watch and Pray

Johann B. Freystein · tr. Catherine Winkworth

STRAF MICH NICHT

1697, cento; Tr. 1863; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Rise, my soul, to watch and pray,from thy sleep awaken;be not by the evil dayunawares o'ertaken.For the foe,well we know,oft his harvest reapethwhile the Christian sleepeth.

Stanza 2

Watch against the devil's snares,lest asleep he find thee;for indeed no pains he sparesto deceive and blind thee.Satan's preyoft are theywho secure are sleepingand no watch are keeping.

Stanza 3

Watch! Let not the wicked worldwith its pow'r defeat thee.Watch lest with her pomp unfurledshe betray and cheat thee.Watch and seelest there befaithless friends to charm thee,who but seek to harm thee.

Stanza 4

Watch against thyself, my soul,lest with grace thou trifle;let not self thy thoughts controlnor God's mercy stifle.Pride and sinlurk withinall thy hopes to scatter;heed not when they flatter.

Stanza 5

But while watching, also prayto the Lord unceasing.He will free thee, be thy stay,strength and faith increasing.O Lord, blessin distressand let nothing swerve mefrom the will to serve thee.
