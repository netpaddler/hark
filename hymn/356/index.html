No. 356

How Beautiful the Sight

James Montgomery

ST. GODRIC · John B. Dykes (1823-1876)

1771-1854

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

How beautiful the sightof brethren who agreein friendship to unite,and bonds of charity;'tis like the precious ointment, shedo'er all his robes, from Aaron's head.

Stanza 2

'Tis like the dew that fillsthe cups of Hermon's flow'rs;or Zion's fruitful hill,bright with the drops of show'rs,when mingling odors breathe around,and glory rests on all the ground.

Stanza 3

For there the Lord commandsblessings, a boundless store,from his unsparing hands,yea, life forevermore:thrice happy they who meet aboveto spend eternity in love!
