No. 211

God Rest You Merry, Gentlemen

English carol, 18th cent.

GOD REST YOU MERRY · English melody, 18th cent.

Themes: Christmas, The Incarnation, Christ's Deity, Joy and Praise, Angels and Shepherds, Grace and Redemption

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

God rest you merry, gentlemen,let nothing you dismay,remember Christ our Saviorwas born on Christmas day,to save us all from Satan's pow'rwhen we were gone astray;

Refrain

O tidings of comfort and joy, comfort and joy,O tidings of comfort and joy.

Stanza 2

From God our heav'nly Father,a blessed angel came;and unto certain shepherdsbrought tidings of the same:how that in Bethlehem was bornthe son of God by name.

Refrain

O tidings of comfort and joy, comfort and joy,O tidings of comfort and joy.

Stanza 3

"Fear not, then," said the angel,"let nothing you affright;this day is born a Saviorof a pure virgin bright,to free all those who trust in himfrom Satan's pow'r and might."

Refrain

O tidings of comfort and joy, comfort and joy,O tidings of comfort and joy.

Stanza 4

The shepherds at those tidingsrejoiced much in mind,and left their flocks a-feeding,in tempest, storm, and wind:and went to Bethlehem straightway,the Son of God to find.

Refrain

O tidings of comfort and joy, comfort and joy,O tidings of comfort and joy.
