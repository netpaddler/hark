No. 602

O God, My Faithful God

Johann Heermann, 1585-1647 · tr. Catherine Winkworth

DARMSTADT (or WAS FRAG' ICH NACH DER WELT) · Ahasuerus Fritsch

1863; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

O God, my faithful God,true fountain ever flowing,without whom nothing is,all perfect gifts bestowing:give me a healthy frame,and may I have withina conscience free from blame,a soul unstained by sin.

Stanza 2

Give me the strength to dowith ready heart and willingwhatever you command,my calling here fulfilling—to do it when I ought,with all my strength; and blesswhatever I have wrought,for you must give success.

Stanza 3

Keep me from saying wordsthat later need recalling;guard me, lest idle speechmay from my lips be falling:but when, within my place,I must and ought to speak,then to my words give grace,lest I offend the weak.

Stanza 4

When dangers gather round,oh, keep me calm and fearless;help me to bear the crosswhen life seems dark and cheerless;help me, as you have taught,to love both great and small,and, by your Spirit's might,to live at peace with all.
