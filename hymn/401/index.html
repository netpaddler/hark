No. 401

All Praise to Thee, My God, This Night

Thomas Ken

TALLIS' CANON · Thomas Tallis (ca. 1567)

1695, 1709

Themes: All Year, Evening, Joy and Praise

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

All praise to thee, my God, this night,for all the blessings of the light;keep me, O keep me, King of kings,beneath thine own almighty wings.

Stanza 2

Forgive me, Lord, for thy dear Son,the ills that I this day have done;that with the world, myself, and thee,I, ere I sleep, at peace may be.

Stanza 3

O may my soul on thee repose,and with sweet sleep mine eyelids close;sleep that may me more vig'rous maketo serve my God when I awake.

Stanza 4

When in the night I sleepless lie,my soul with heav'nly thoughts supply;let no ill dreams disturb my rest,no pow'rs of darkness me molest.

Stanza 5

O when shall I in endless dayforever chase dark sleep away,and hymns with the supernal choirincessant sing, and never tire!

Stanza 6

Praise God from whom all blessings flow;praise him, all creatures here below;praise him above, ye heav'nly host:praise Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.
