No. 206

Brightest and Best of the Sons of the Morning

Reginald Heber

MORNING STAR · James P. Harding

text 1811; Alt. 1961, mod.

Themes: Christmas, Epiphany, Christ's Deity, Christ the King, Light in Darkness, The Magi and the Nations

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Brightest and best of the sons of the morning,dawn on our darkness and lend us your aid;Star of the East, the horizon adorning,guide where our infant Redeemer is laid.

Stanza 2

Cold on his cradle the dewdrops are shining;low lies his head with the beasts of the stall;angels adore him in slumber reclining,Maker and Monarch and Lord over all.

Stanza 3

Say, shall we yield him, in costly devotion,odors of Edom, and off'rings divine,gems of the mountain and pearls of the ocean,myrrh from the forest or gold from the mine?

Stanza 4

Vainly we offer each ample oblation,vainly with gifts would his favor secure;richer by far is the heart's adoration;dearer to God are the prayers of the poor.

Stanza 5

Brightest and best of the sons of the morning,dawn on our darkness and lend us your aid;Star of the East, the horizon adorning,guide where our infant Redeemer is laid.
