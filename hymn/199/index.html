No. 199

See, amid the Winter's Snow

Edward Caswall

SEE AMID THE WINTER'S SNOW · John Goss

text 1851

Themes: Christmas, The Incarnation, Christ's Deity, Angels and Shepherds

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

See, amid the winter's snow,born for us on earth below,see the tender Lamb appears,promised from eternal years.

Refrain

Hail, thou ever-blessed morn!Hail, redemption's happy dawn!Sing through all Jerusalem,Christ is born in Bethlehem.

Stanza 2

Lo, within a manger lieshe who built the starry skies:he who, throned in height sublime,sits amid the cherubim.

Refrain

Hail, thou ever-blessed morn!Hail, redemption's happy dawn!Sing through all Jerusalem,Christ is born in Bethlehem.

Stanza 3

Say, ye holy shepherds, say,what's your joyful news today?Wherefore have ye left your sheepon the lonely mountain steep?

Refrain

Hail, thou ever-blessed morn!Hail, redemption's happy dawn!Sing through all Jerusalem,Christ is born in Bethlehem.

Stanza 4

"As we watched at dead of night,lo, we saw a wondrous light;angels singing, 'Peace on earth,'told us of the Savior's birth."

Refrain

Hail, thou ever-blessed morn!Hail, redemption's happy dawn!Sing through all Jerusalem,Christ is born in Bethlehem.

Stanza 5

Sacred infant, all divine,what a tender love was thine,thus to come from highest blissdown to such a world as this!

Refrain

Hail, thou ever-blessed morn!Hail, redemption's happy dawn!Sing through all Jerusalem,Christ is born in Bethlehem.

Stanza 6

Teach, O teach us, holy child,by thy face so meek and mild,teach us to resemble thee,in thy sweet humility.

Refrain

Hail, thou ever-blessed morn!Hail, redemption's happy dawn!Sing through all Jerusalem,Christ is born in Bethlehem.
