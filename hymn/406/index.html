No. 406

Now the Day Is Over

Sabine Baring-Gould

MERRIAL · Joseph Barnby (1868)

1865

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Now the day is over,night is drawing nigh,shadows of the eveningsteal across the sky.

Stanza 2

Jesus, give the wearycalm and sweet repose;with thy tend'rest blessingmay our eyelids close.

Stanza 3

Grant to little childrenvisions bright of thee;guard the sailors, tossingon the deep blue sea.

Stanza 4

Comfort ev'ry suff'rerwatching late in pain;those who plan some evilfrom their sin restrain.

Stanza 5

Through the long night watchesmay thine angels spreadtheir white wings above me,watching round my bed.

Stanza 6

Glory to the Father,glory to the Son,and to thee, blest Spirit,whilst all ages run.
