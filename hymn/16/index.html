No. 16

Come, Let Us Sing unto the Lord

Associate Reformed Presbyterian Psalter

DUKE STREET · John Hatton (1793)

1931; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Come, let us sing unto the Lordnew songs of praise with sweet accord;for wonders great by him are done,his hand and arm have vict'ry won.

Stanza 2

The great salvation of our Godis seen through all the earth abroad;before the heathen's wond'ring sighthe has revealed his truth and right.

Stanza 3

He called to mind his truth and gracein promise made to Israel's race;and unto earth's remotest boundglad tidings of salvation sound.

Stanza 4

All lands, to God lift up your voice;sing praise to him, with shouts rejoice;with voice of joy and loud acclaimlet all unite and praise his name.

Stanza 5

Praise God with harp, with harp sing praise,with voice of psalms his glory raise;with trumpets, cornets, gladly singand shout before the Lord, the King.

Stanza 6

Let earth be glad, let billows roarand all that dwell from shore to shore;let floods clap hands with one accord,let hills rejoice before the Lord.

Stanza 7

For lo, he comes; at his commandall nations shall in judgment stand;in justice robed and throned in light,the Lord shall judge, dispensing right.
