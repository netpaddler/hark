No. 99

My Song Forever Shall Record

The Psalter, 1912

WINCHESTER NEW (1990 Trinity Hymnal) · also sung to MARYTON (1961 Trinity Hymnal)

Psalm 89:1-4, 14-18, 28, 29; mod.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

My song forever shall recordthe tender mercies of the Lord;your faithfulness will I proclaim,and every age shall know your name.

Stanza 2

I sing of mercies that endure,forever builded firm and sure,of faithfulness that never dies,established changeless in the skies.

Stanza 3

Behold God's truth and grace displayed,for he has faithful cov'nant made,and he has sworn that David's sonshall ever sit upon his throne:

Stanza 4

"For him my mercy shall endure,my cov'nant made with him is sure;his throne and race I will maintainforever, while the heav'ns remain."

Stanza 5

Almighty God, your lofty thronehas justice for its cornerstone,and shining bright before your faceare truth and love and boundless grace.

Stanza 6

With blessing is the nation crownedwhose people know the joyful sound;they in the light, O Lord, shall live,the light your face and favor give.

Stanza 7

Your name with gladness they confess,exalted in your righteousness;their fame and might to you belong,for in your favor they are strong.

Stanza 8

All glory unto God we yield,Jehovah is our help and shield;all praise and honor we will bringto Israel's Holy One, our King.
