No. 348

Jesus, with Thy Church Abide

Thomas Benson Pollock

GOWER'S LITANY · John Henry Gower (1891)

1871; alt. in Hymns Ancient and Modern, 1875

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Jesus, with thy church abide,be her Savior, Lord, and Guide,while on earth her faith is tried:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 2

Keep her life and doctrine pure;grant her patience to endure,trusting in thy promise sure:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 3

May she one in doctrine be,one in truth and charity,winning all to faith in thee:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 4

May she guide the poor and blind,seek the lost until she find,and the broken-hearted bind:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 5

Save her love from growing cold,make her watchmen strong and bold,fence her round, thy peaceful fold:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 6

May her lamp of truth be bright,bid her bear aloft its lightthrough the realms of heathen night:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 7

Arm her soldiers with the cross,brave to suffer toil or loss,counting earthly gain but dross:we beseech thee, hear us.

Stanza 8

May she holy triumphs win,overthrow the hosts of sin,gather all the nations in:we beseech thee, hear us.
