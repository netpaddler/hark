No. 700

Come, We That Love the Lord

Isaac Watts

ST. THOMAS · Aaron Williams

1707; alt.

Themes: All Year

Open in Hark

Stanza 1

Come, we that love the Lord,and let our joys be known;join in a song with sweet accord,and thus surround the throne.

Stanza 2

Let those refuse to singthat never knew our God;but children of the heav'nly Kingmay speak their joys abroad.

Stanza 3

The men of grace have foundglory begun below;celestial fruits on earthly groundfrom faith and hope may grow.

Stanza 4

The hill of Zion yieldsa thousand sacred sweets,before we reach the heav'nly fieldsor walk the golden streets.

Stanza 5

Then let our songs abound,and ev'ry tear be dry;we're marching through Immanuel's groundto fairer worlds on high.
